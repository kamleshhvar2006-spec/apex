One carrier for the whole supply chain

Most shipments cross several hands between pickup and delivery. We own the booking, the routing and the status updates, so you deal with one team and one reference number.

From a single pallet to Odisha to full container loads out of Paradeep, we plan the cheapest mode that still meets your delivery date.

  • Live shipment status

    Every handoff updates the shipment record. Customers see current location and delivery date without calling us.

  • Customs-ready paperwork

    Export and import documents are prepared with the booking, so cargo is not held at the port for missing forms.

  • Door-to-door delivery

    First-mile pickup and last-mile delivery are part of the quote, not an add-on you discover later.

  • Quotes in rupees, GST included

    The estimate shows freight, fuel surcharge and GST as separate lines, so finance can check it.

Network overview

Ten Indian hubs feed nine international gateways. Red lines are our main lanes.

India: 10 hubs

Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Bhubaneswar, Paradeep, Visakhapatnam and Kochi. Road lanes connect all of them; six have seaports.

Asia and Middle East

Singapore, Dubai, Shanghai and Hong Kong, by ocean weekly and by air daily.

Europe and North America

Rotterdam, London, Frankfurt, Los Angeles and New York, linked through Dubai and Singapore.

Services

Road freight

Full and part truckloads between Indian cities, with first-mile pickup. Best for 1 to 40 tonnes.

Typical transit: 2 to 7 days

Ocean freight

FCL and LCL container shipping between port cities, including customs clearance at both ends.

Typical transit: 12 to 30 days

Air cargo

Time-critical freight on scheduled cargo flights, from a few kilograms up to 5 tonnes.

Typical transit: 2 to 5 days

Port and warehousing

Container handling, bonded storage and cross-docking at our Paradeep and Mumbai yards.
